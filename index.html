HOW THE ARCADE WORKS

One game, one save, one currency — molecules you earn in any mode spend in every mode. Swipe the hero above to pick a game — walk, dig, build, explore, fight, loot. Your house is home base, the dungeon is under it, and every other game is a place you walk to. This menu is the hub — tap ☐ MODES in the top bar any time to jump anywhere. Tip: open your browser menu and choose "Add to Home Screen" / "Install app" to keep the arcade on your home screen.
