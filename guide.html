Everything is free to play — no game asks you to pay to enter. Play any game to earn molecules; they spend in every game.

Stack atoms with !daily in the Tavern (+50 atoms a day) and build up from there. Atoms open revives, pets, skins — and the Exchange at 500.

THE TAVERN

The Tavern is the chat room, the Keeper's shop, and the coin purse. The Keeper sells the looks — name colors, rings, and flames. Guess the Drawing and Movie Night happen here too. Type commands in the chat — !help lists them all.

LINK YOUR WALLET

Your ATOM1- code comes from the Tavern coin purse (Tavern → YOUR COIN PURSE → copy the code) or from an atom purchase. Paste it into the ATOM WALLET box on the hub and tap LINK. Never share it — it spends your atoms.

REVIVES

A revive costs 3 ions / 15 atoms / 10 molecules. Revives exist only in the 7 run games: Dino Run, SAV, Slimeball, Star Invader, Tetris, Wraith Wings, Wraith Serpent. Newer games have no revives — Pinball has a 5-second ball-save instead.

THE GAMES

💀 VANIX FIGHT

1V1 · 2V2 · 3V3 · Showdown · Waves · Defense. 28 fighters, one balance table. Specials cost 10 mana (max 100, refills 4/sec). Kavio's ghost block phases through ranged shots — you'll see PHASED! GRIM is 1V1-only: beat the boss to unlock him. Fill your meter for the WRAITH VERDICT finisher. Controls: D-pad + buttons — punch · kick · block · special.

⛏️ DELVE

Dig down deep. Fight, loot, and climb back out. 7 heroes: Kynth, Wrightsmith, Nightwraith1, Goldraven, Kavio, Balloon Guy, Lestes — Lestes is the only shapeshifter. Heroes cost 1–5 ions, cycling. Controls: D-pad + buttons — dig · fight · loot.

💾 SAV

The original SAV arcade game. Old school, fast, and mean. Controls: D-pad + buttons.

🐾 WRAITH PET

Your buddy lives in your house. Feed it, play, watch it grow. Pack battles: your pets are cards — you start with 3 energy, gain +2 each turn (max 10). Pets: Ember 200 / Frost 275 / Shadow 350 / Morph 425 / Apex 500 molecules — or Ember $1 / Frost $2 / Shadow $3 / Morph $4 (Apex stays locked). Controls: Tap + buttons — feed · play · grow.

🧱 TETRIS

Stack the blocks, clear the lines. Line clears build a 3×3 blast — 10 lines = 1 blast. Pops mint no candy (score only). Controls: D-pad + rotate.

👾 STAR INVADER

Shoot the invaders out of the sky before they land. Shoot the flashing gold FLASH SHOT capsule for ~10 seconds of 3-way spread fire. Controls: D-pad + fire.

🫐 SLIMEBALL

You're the slime. Bounce, smash, and devour everything. Beating a creature means the slime eats it and gains its power: Cinderkit first-attack flare, Pebblit stone skin, Bloomimp healing drain, Galewing double-strike chance. Tilt to roll — the roll is proportional to how far you tilt. Controls: Tilt or D-pad.

🃏 WRAITH CARDS

Flip the pairs and match them all. Every match feeds your collection. Best = fewest moves to clear the board. Controls: Tap to flip.

🕹️ PINBALL

Classic pinball — flip, bounce, and chase the high score. KAVIO / VANIX / WRAITH bumpers +100. Light all 4 S·A·V·E targets for +1000 and ×2 scoring for 10 seconds. 3 balls, with a 5-second ball-save after each launch. Molecules banked at the end: 1 per 1000 points, max 50. Tap ▶ START on the table to launch. Controls: Tap/hold screen halves or flipper buttons · ←/Z + →// flippers · ↓/Space plunger · gamepad LB/LT + A.

CHARACTERS

The characters live on the main site: swpvp-dsu8.vercel.app — CHARACTERS in the nav. Tap a card for the bigger view + lore. Two things to know: Kynth is the main guy overall. Kavio is the face and mascot.
