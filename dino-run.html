DINO RUN
GOAL — Run as far as you can down THREE LANES while the cave collapses behind you. Switch lanes to pick a path, jump and slide the rest.
CONTROLS — ← → or A / D to switch lanes · ▲ JUMP button, Space, ↑ or W to jump · ▼ SLIDE button, ↓ or S to slide · CHOMP button, X, F or C to chomp. On touch: swipe left / right to switch lanes, swipe up / down to jump / slide, or use the retro pad.
HOW IT PLAYS — JUMP clears spikes, gaps and walls. SLIDE smashes boulders and ducks under sweepers and flyers. CHOMP eats ground chasers. Grab the gold bolt for RUSH — smash through everything for a few seconds. Coins are +25, gems +100, eating a chaser is +250, smashing in RUSH is +150. You have 3 lives — but if the COLLAPSE catches you, the run ends no matter how many lives are left.
SCORING — Score grows with distance + bonuses. Your BEST score is saved on this device.
