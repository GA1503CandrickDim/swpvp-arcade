WRAITH DELVE — HOW TO PLAY
GOAL — Room by room: clear the room, grab the sigil, take the stairs down. The Keeper guards every 3rd floor. Escape the Hollow: find 3 sigils and reach the gold door.

MOVE — ◀ ▶ or A/D to run, ▲ or W to JUMP. Side view — gravity pulls you down, so jump and climb. SPACE or 1 = strike, Q or 2 = skill, X or 3 = MINE, Z = place a block, C = build, V = farm, T = throw, B = barricade, G = craft, SHIFT = sneak. P pauses. On touch, use the arrow pad (▲ = jump) and skill tiles.

MINING — Punch a block a few times and it cracks, then breaks and drops its goods. X mines the block in front of you; hold DOWN + X to mine the block under your feet and dig straight down. Deeper = harder + rarer: SOFT DIRT up top, STONE, DEEP STONE, ORE ROCK, and SHARD ROCK in the deep dark. Dig up ore, craft a WRAITH PICK at the workbench to mine 2x faster, and place blocks (Z) to build stairs and climb back out.

HEROES — Pick 1 of 7 before you delve: KYNTH, WRIGHTSMITH, NIGHTWRAITH1, GOLDRAVEN, KAVIO, BALLOON GUY, LESTES. Every hero has their own skill (Q / 2). LESTES is the shapeshifter — BELT SHIFT spins his belt spheres into BEAR, WOLF, HAWK and FIREFLY forms. Use all 4 to summon the AEGIS MECHA.

A RUN — One run = one dungeon seed. Same seed = same dungeon. Waves spawn on every floor. Combo kills multiply your score, kills pay molecules. Hunger and fuel always drain — eat, craft, hide. When you fall, the run ends; your best score and deepest floor are saved on this device.
